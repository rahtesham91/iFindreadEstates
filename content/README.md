# Company content taken from https://ifindrealestates.com

Crawled on 2026-10-03. The old site is WordPress with the Houzez theme and its sitemap lists about 300 URLs. Most of them are theme demo material, so only content that really belongs to the company is kept here.

## What is here
| Folder / file | Contents |
|---|---|
| `company/profile.md` | Contact details, address, working hours, social links, old menu, tagline, and where they differ from the new details |
| `company/about.md` | Company introduction, Founder & CEO text (Mr. Avaid Lateef), services wording |
| `company/home.md` | Old home page wording |
| `company/developers.md` | The 12 developers and their logo images |
| `company/areas.md` | Popular areas and listing categories |
| `team/team.md` | The 10 team members with roles and bios, plus the 4 agent bios |
| `properties/` | The 7 real listings (overview, description, address, details, features, images) |
| `blog/` | The 4 genuine blog posts |
| `media/company-media.md` | 93 company images/files uploaded to the media library from 2025 onward, with URLs and where they are used |

## What was left out, and why
- 170 `houzez_invoice` URLs: numbered `invoice-1` to `invoice-170`, dated 2020. Theme demo data and billing records.
- 12 blog posts, FAQ, Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, Contact page: lorem ipsum or Houzez placeholder text (Miami address, `@houzez.com` emails).
- 4 testimonials and 6 "Envato" partners: theme demo.
- About 35 layout demo pages (grid/list/map/typography/packages/my-profile and so on) and the Houzez header/footer templates.
- Agent licence numbers, phone numbers, languages, service areas and reviews: theme demo values.
- Media uploaded in 2016-2020 (about 110 files): theme demo images; Houzez placeholders and the demo-import log.
- Taxonomy and archive pages: no unique content beyond the lists in `company/areas.md`.

## Things to confirm with the company before reuse
1. "Over 8 years of experience", "12+ years of sales experience" and similar claims.
2. Services wording such as "Property Management" (conflicts with the missing management licence).
3. Listing prices and details are from 2025 and may be out of date. One listing shows a price of 7,920 AED for a 4BR sky villa, which looks like an entry error.
